About Us

Building better tools

BLOUplanet is a developer from South Korea. Started coding in 2020 with Arduino, began vibe coding in 2023, and has been building many open-source projects since.

Operating multiple services centered around bloupla.net, and has released several AI models on Hugging Face. Every project starts from fun, curiosity, or frustration — and is developed with the goal of making things easier for everyone.
