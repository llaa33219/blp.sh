3. Use of Cookies

This site may use cookies for user convenience. Cookies are small pieces of data sent by a web server to the user's browser and stored on the user's computer.

Purpose of Cookies

  • Analyzing visit frequency and usage patterns to improve services
  • Serving personalized advertisements (Google AdSense)

How to Reject Cookies

Users can refuse or delete cookies through their browser settings. However, rejecting cookies may limit access to some services.

This site may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on user interests. For more information, please visit Google Ads Policies .

Users can disable personalized ads in their Google Ads Settings .

The operator does not, in principle, share users' personal information with third parties. However, exceptions are made in the following cases:

  • When the user has given prior consent

Collected personal information is deleted without delay once the purpose of collection is fulfilled. Automatically collected log data is retained for a maximum of one year before deletion.
